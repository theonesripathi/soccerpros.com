SOCCER PROS

You are Blue and attack to the right. Win the match in 2 minutes.

Controls

Move

←↑↓→ or WASD. You control the player nearest the ball.

Pass

Space or J. Hold a direction to pass to the teammate that way.

Shoot

Hold K or X to charge, release to shoot. Hold up or down to aim at a post.

Sprint

Hold Shift to run faster. The bar under your player shows stamina and refills when you ease off.

Skill move

L or C. A quick spin burst that beats tackles. Short cooldown.

Set pieces

Throw-ins

When the ball crosses the sideline, the other team throws it in. Aim with a direction and press Pass.

Goal kicks

If the attackers put it over the end line, the keeper restarts play.

Corners

If the defenders put it over their own end line, the attackers take a corner.
